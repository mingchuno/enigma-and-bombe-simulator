# Enigma and Bombe

Terms for enciphering messages and checking possible settings against a crib.

## Language

**Crib**: Suspected plaintext aligned with part of an intercepted ciphertext.

**Crib menu**: Letter connections imposed by a crib and its ciphertext alignment, each tied to a message position.

**Bombe search session**: One attempt to find settings compatible with a crib, with known rings and reflector. A stopped session retains partial results; replacing the intercept or search assumptions starts a fresh attempt.

**Candidate**: A rotor order, starting windows and witness plugboard that satisfy the crib. It is not a uniquely recovered key; letters unconstrained by the crib remain unknown.
