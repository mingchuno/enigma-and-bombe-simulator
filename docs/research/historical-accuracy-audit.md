# Historical accuracy audit

Reviewed 6 September 2026: `src/engine/enigma.ts`, `bombe.ts`, `historical.ts`, the Drums, Historical Menu, Paper Methods and Guide components, existing research, and relevant tests. This is a code/source audit, not a certification of physical Bombe fidelity. Wartime documents are distinguished below from museum custodians' measurements/reconstruction accounts and independent software fixtures.

## Verified behavior

- **Enigma wiring and conventions:** rotor I–V permutations, reflector B/C permutations, and window turnover letters Q/E/V/J/Z match David Hamer's published wiring table. Hamer distinguishes physical notch location from the turnover letter visible in the window; the code uses the latter correctly. Wide reflector C is distinct from thin naval C. [Hamer's wiring measurements](https://enigmamuseum.com/rotwirg.htm)
- **Stepping and rings:** stepping before electrical evaluation, simultaneous notch tests, middle double stepping, and the `position − ring` electrical offset are consistent with the mechanism. Changing rings does not change the visible turnover letter because the notch is attached to the alphabet ring. The documented III–II–I sequence KDQ → KER → LFS is correct. The physical plugboard accepts up to **13** cables; **10** was usual operational practice. Allowing 13 is not a defect. [Crypto Museum's mechanism and hardware description](https://www.cryptomuseum.com/crypto/enigma/working.htm)
- **Bombe structure and current propagation:** a vertical set of three drums is one double-ended scrambler; three chains of twelve give 36 scramblers and 108 drums. Duplicate rotor wiring provides separate outward/return circuits. The graph's `(A,b) ↔ (B,a)` reciprocal connections match the diagonal board. Rejecting when all 26 register wires are energized, and calling any smaller count a *potential* stop, matches the general sensing condition. Restricting stops to precisely one or 25 live wires would incorrectly exclude ambiguous stops. [TNMOC rebuild machine description](https://www.tnmoc.org/bh-10-bombe-description)
- **Drum rows and drive:** top/fast search drum corresponds to Enigma's left rotor; bottom/slow search drum corresponds to its right rotor and carries relative message offsets. The 39-point variant performs 26 sensing points and 13 unsensed carry points. Three top revolutions per two middle steps is correct. `driveState` covers all 17,576 normalized orientations once during sensing: each middle/bottom pair receives all 26 top values; alternating blocks start 13 top positions apart. The latter is a mathematical check of the code, not a measured phase calibration. [Virtual Bombe author's technical account](https://bombe.virtualcolossus.co.uk/technical.html)
- **Menu and checking principles:** the wartime report documents relative settings referred to ZZZ, 26-way reciprocal wiring, three banks of twelve scramblers, 60 orders, and checking stops separately. It also describes special turnover/hoppity menus. Consequently the app's separation between the exact Enigma search and the restricted historical core demonstration is warranted. A million-position sweep is not the complete unknown-key problem. [6812th Signal Security Detachment report, printed pp. 4–6, 12–16, 37–38](https://www.codesandciphers.org.uk/documents/bmbrpt/usbmbrpt.pdf)
- **ZZZ notation:** `relativeLabel(0) = ZZA`, position 12 = ZZL, and position 26 = ZZZ follow the declared pre-first-character reference. Returning `+27` beyond that avoids asserting a turnover. The tutorial also preserves parallel G–R links at positions 6 and 12, chooses input G, and removes S–W to reduce its final menu to twelve links. The app's thirteen-edge transcription explicitly warns about capacity. [Virtual Bombe tutorial, PDF pp. 6–8](https://bombe.virtualcolossus.co.uk/bombe/VirtualTuringWelchmanBombeTutorial.pdf)
- **Banbury demonstration:** comparing two ciphertexts at a relative shift and counting coincident holes matches the wartime definition of placing messages in depth. Coincidences provide statistical evidence, not decrypted plaintext or a proof that a shift is correct. The component states this distinction accurately. [1944 Bletchley Park cryptographic dictionary, p. 5](https://www.codesandciphers.org.uk/documents/cryptdict/page05.htm)
- **Zygalski distinction:** the alternative description correctly connects perforated candidate-setting grids to repetitions in doubled message keys, rather than treating them as Bombe cribs. Identifying a remembered exhibit as Banbury remains an explicitly labelled inference. [Science Museum reconstruction researchers' account](https://journal.sciencemuseum.ac.uk/article/zygalski-sheets-polish-codebreaking-and-the-role-of-reconstruction-in-the-top-secret-exhibition-at-the-science-museum/)

## Teaching simplifications to retain explicitly

1. The drums show ring-A core coordinates, not original engraved letters or stop-indicator readings. Rotor-specific historical drum offsets are deliberately not applied. Schematic contact geometry, rotation direction, boundary-only carry motion and omitted braking are already disclosed. Do not remove these disclosures when polishing the presentation.
2. The electrical display is graph reachability at a fixed orientation. It does not reproduce sense/stop-and-cancel relay timing or automatically halt the animation. One-live and 25-live patterns describe familiar good-stop behavior; other partial patterns still require checking. [Bombe rebuild participant Frank Carter's account](https://www.rutherfordjournal.org/article030108.html)
3. Twelve-edge pages are independently sensed teaching subsets. They are not a full-menu circuit or additional physical banks; the existing page warning is necessary.
4. `historicalScramblers` adds position offsets only to the right-equivalent core and assumes no relevant turnover across the menu. Offsets at or beyond a full revolution remain schematic. `searchBombe` instead enumerates starting windows with known rings/reflector and obtains scramblers by real Enigma stepping.
5. `compareStrips` performs literal equality and overlap counting. It does not implement Banburismus likelihood scoring, wheel-order deduction or message-network analysis.

## Actionable findings and limits

**Candidate certainty language needs correction.** `solveMenu` returns the first satisfying partial involution for each window/order. Self-pairs are attempted first. Assigned pairs are therefore one compatible witness, not necessarily pairings logically forced by every solution. The solver also does not enumerate every compatible plugboard for that orientation. Wording such as “forced pairs” or “all letters constrained” overstates recovery unless uniqueness has separately been proved. Use “compatible pairs” and “one compatible assignment”; unresolved letters outside the assigned witness remain unknown. This is established directly from the branch/return implementation, independently of historical sources. The parent audit was notified for implementation.

**Independent reflector-C coverage was missing in the reviewed tests.** The wiring matches Hamer, but the original end-to-end fixtures exercise B. Generated the following interoperability fixtures using unmodified Py-Enigma **1.0.2** in an isolated temporary environment; these are software cross-checks, not wartime messages. [Library author's tests](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/gremmie/enigma/master/enigma/tests/test_enigma.py), [library author's rotor/reflector data](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/gremmie/enigma/master/enigma/rotors/data.py)

**No concrete historical algorithm defect found** in the reviewed Enigma stepping/ring implementation, 39-point enumeration, row mapping, ZZZ offset convention, reachability rules, or paper comparison. This finding is limited to the declared teaching scope. Original supplied photographs were not available in this delegated audit, so their exact transcription is not independently certified; the published tutorial corroborates the relationships noted above.
